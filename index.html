📖 Seller Tutorial: HOODLOOP Store

Learn how to easily add clothing photos, change prices, toggle Sold Out badges, or delete items.

1. How to Add New Clothes

• Click the "Manage Store" button in the navigation header.
• Enter your apparel title (e.g., HOODLOOP Retro Shorts).
• Price is pre-filled at ₱450.00 (you can change it if needed).
• Choose a photo of your clothes from your smartphone or computer, then click Add Item!

2. How to Mark an Item as "SOLD OUT"

• Open Manage Store.
• Scroll to Manage Inventory.
• Click the "Mark Sold Out" button next to any item.
• A bold "SOLD OUT" badge will overlay on the image and disable purchasing for customers. You can click it again anytime to mark it back In Stock.

3. How to Delete / Remove Products

• Open Manage Store.
• Click the red "Delete" button next to the product you want to remove.

4. Automatic Saving

• All products, prices, sold-out statuses, and uploaded images are automatically saved in your browser's local memory (LocalStorage).
